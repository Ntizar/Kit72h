The 5 typical mistakes (and how to avoid them)

  1. Not checking the V-16 beacon: the connection expires (it usually lasts about 12 years from manufacture) and some models are not DGT-approved. Triangles are no longer valid from 1/1/2026.
  2. Having the kit in the boot and the boot full of stuff from the last move. A covered-up first-aid kit doesn't exist.
  3. Not checking the spare wheel or the water level: a puncture 30 km from the nearest petrol station is a problem that lasts hours.
  4. Relying on your phone with 8% battery and no power bank in the car.
  5. In snow, setting off without chains or not knowing how to fit them. Textile snow socks are a legal stopgap in some places and a danger in others: find out beforehand what your area allows.

Quick guide

→ Before

  • Check tires, battery and fluid levels before long trips
  • Check the condition of the kit at every change of season

→ During

  • If you break down, signal from inside the car with the approved connected V-16 beacon: it is the only legal means from 1/1/2026 (RD 159/2021; DGT, note of 25/11/2025). Triangles are no longer valid
  • Do not get out of the car in snow if there is traffic: inside is safer and warmer
  • From 1/10/2026, when passing a vehicle stopped due to a breakdown or accident, reduce the road speed limit by at least 20 km/h and leave 1.5 m of lateral separation; in traffic jams, open a central emergency lane for ambulances and tow trucks (RD 518/2026, of 24 June, BOE-A-2026-13889, published 26/06/2026)
  • From 1/10/2026, wearing a seat belt is always mandatory: the reform of the General Traffic Regulations removes the exemptions for taxi drivers, delivery drivers and driving school instructors; only patients in an ambulance on emergency service are exempt (RD 518/2026, art. 119.2, BOE-A-2026-13889)
  • From 1/10/2026, on motorways and dual carriageways where the road is made difficult by snow, overtaking is prohibited and you must drive in the right-hand lane (the adjacent one if there are three or more), leaving the left-hand lanes free for snowploughs and emergency services (RD 518/2026, art. 32 of the General Traffic Regulations, BOE-A-2026-13889)

→ After

  • Restock what you have used (batteries, water, fuel) when you get home
