The 5 typical mistakes (and how to avoid them)

  1. Buying panels without an inverter or batteries. Panels only generate with sun: without storage you have nothing at night. Batteries and inverter first, then panels.
  2. Installing lead-acid batteries in 2026. LFP lithium costs 3–4× more but lasts 5× longer (3,000+ cycles vs 500–1,000): it works out cheaper per cycle.
  3. Putting Starlink where the sky is obstructed. It needs ~100° of clear sky; trees, roofs or mountains block the signal. Roof or terrace with an open view.
  4. Running the generator indoors. CO is invisible and odourless and kills in minutes: always outdoors, ventilated, 3+ metres from windows and doors.
  5. Skipping a professional installation. Poorly anchored panels fly off, badly connected batteries catch fire, unprotected cables melt. €200–500 for a certified installation is worth every cent.

Quick guide

→ Before

  • Calculate your daily consumption: kWh/month ÷ 30 = kWh/day. The kit should cover 70-80% of that consumption.
  • Identify where to install the panels: south-facing roof, flat roof or adjacent land. Minimum 4 m² for every 400 W.
  • Prepare the battery space: ventilated area, 10-25 °C, away from flammable materials.
  • Locate the generator spot: outdoors, ventilated, 3+ metres from windows, with access for refuelling.
  • If you don't know how to install it: budget for an electrician + certified solar installer. It's not witchcraft, but it has to be done right.

→ During

  • Batteries and inverter first, then panels, then Starlink. Test each module before connecting the next one.
  • Check polarity before connecting: negative to negative, positive to positive. A short circuit in batteries is instant.
  • Set the inverter's low-voltage cutoff (11.5 V on 12 V; 40 V on 48 V) so deep discharges don't damage the batteries.
  • Activate Starlink and its subscription. Run a speed test; adjust the position if latency is high or packets are being lost.
  • Test the generator outdoors: 30 minutes under load, fuel stabilized.

→ After

  • Clean the panels every 2–3 months: dust, fallen leaves and bird droppings cut efficiency by 20–30%.
  • Check connections and fuses every 6 months: corrosion, loose screws and frayed cables cause most failures.
  • Battery test once a month: voltage and temperature. A cell that runs hotter than the rest is going to fail.
  • Test the generator every 3 months (30 min under load). Don't leave petrol in the tank for more than 6 months.
  • Renew your Starlink plan before it expires: prices sometimes change when you reactivate.
