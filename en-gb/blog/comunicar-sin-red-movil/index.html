Communicating without a network: what works when mobile service goes down (2026)

📅 2026-08-31 · ⏱ 2 min min read

On 28 April 2025 it became clear: when everyone tries to call at once, the network goes down. And during a DANA, antennas can be left without power or fibre for days. If your family plan depends on WhatsApp, you don't have a plan. Real alternatives, from free to cheap:

1. SMS and short messages

SMS uses a minimal signal that works when calls and data don't. Agree with your family: if the network is struggling, don't call — send a short SMS with your status and location.

2. The out-of-area contact

Classic Civil Protection technique: designate a person who lives far away (another region, another country) as your contact point. Local calls get overloaded; a long-distance or out-of-region call sometimes gets through. Everyone calls that person, and they coordinate.

3. Battery-powered FM/AM radio

It's not for talking, it's for finding out: when telecommunications collapse, broadcasting (large transmitters, their own antennas) is the last information channel still standing. The battery-powered or hand-crank radio is the most underrated item in the kit.

4. PMR446 walkie-talkies

For €30–40 a pair, no licence required in Spain, 2–5 km in the city (further in the countryside). Perfect for coordinating between homes in the same residential complex or on a campsite. Store them with fresh batteries and don't use them as toys.

5. Off-grid messaging

Mesh apps (antenna-free bridges, such as Bluetooth radio links between nearby phones) exist, but they all require both people to have them installed beforehand. Install them now on your family's phones — in the middle of a crisis you can't download anything.

The family plan in one sentence

If there's no network: everyone sends an SMS to the out-of-area contact with “I'm OK / where I am / what I need”, and we meet at the agreed point if 24 hours pass with no contact. Write that point down on paper: under the bell tower, the motorway service station, auntie's house.

The radio and power banks that keep this communication going are in the basic 72h kit and in the power outage kit.
