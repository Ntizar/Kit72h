Best Starlink in Spain: price, plans and which one to choose (2026)

📅 2026-10-15 · ⏱ 6 min min read

Almost every question about Starlink that reaches this blog starts the same way: how much does it really cost, and which plan do I need? In 2026 it changed twice: in March Starlink went from two plans to three, and in June it added 10 €/mes for kit rental for new sign-ups. Here are today's official prices, the comparison, and the verdict for each case.

It is not a niche product: based on network traffic, Starlink would already be the sixth operator in Spain, with more than half a million users, and during the 28 April 2025 blackout, the satellite service was one of the few that kept working. That is the argument of this site: that the connection doesn't disappear just when you need it. The lessons from that day are in the 28 April blackout; the equipment, in the Starlink kit.

1. The five Starlink plans in Spain, compared

Starlink sells two families in Spain: Residential (fixed home) and Mobile (motorhomes, sailboats, use while moving). All include unlimited data and a 30-day trial with a full refund. Prices are those on Starlink's official website for Spain:

Winners by category: the Residential 200 (45 €) is the sweet spot: it doubles the basic speed without paying for Max priority. For a small apartment or second home, the Residential 100 (35 €) is more than enough. The Max (65 €) only pays off with several devices connected at once or real priority at peak times. For a van, the Mobile 100 GB (45 €) with Standby Mode.

A warning: Starlink adjusts prices often (twice in 2026). Check the price at checkout on the official plans website, not the price in an article from six months ago.

2. The equipment: since June 2026 it is rented, not bought

What most people miss: since June 2026, new sign-ups also pay 10 €/mes for the kit (antenna and router):

  • Residential 100: 45 €/mes actual (35 plan fee + 10 kit).
  • Residential 200: 55 €/mes actual (45 + 10).
  • Residential Max: 75 €/mes actual (65 + 10).
  • The 10 € comes to 120 € a year, and the equipment is not yours: if you cancel, it is returned.

Two nuances: a «rental adjustment» often appears that brings those 10 € down to 0, so count the checkout price, not the one in the table; and professional installation is optional, from about 172 €, only for tricky roofs (the app tells you where to point it; anyone can assemble the basic setup).

If you choose only one thing, the Starlink Mini: it fits in a backpack, has an integrated router, runs directly on DC power, and exceeds 200 Mb/s. It is the one that makes sense in an emergency kit; here you can see accessories and mounts for Starlink Mini ↗.

3. Is it worth it in your case? Yes if…, no if…

It is worth it when: fiber doesn't reach you or actually arrives at 20 Mb/s; fixed 5G drops at night; you live in a village or an isolated mountain house; or it is a second home used for weeks at a time (with Standby Mode at about 10 €/mes instead of paying the full rate all year).

It is not worth it when: you have stable, cheap fiber; when you need internet anywhere in the country rather than at one address (that is what mobile data is for); or if you don't want a monthly fee, because the big part of the cost is the monthly one.

And the point almost nobody calculates beforehand: power. A terminal consumes 60-75 W active and 20-40 W idle, so without mains power you have to power it: with a portable power station ↗, with a folding solar panel that recharges during the day or, if it is a fixed installation, with the SAI for the router. A 20,000 mAh power bank is not enough for the antenna. How many watts you really need: portable solar panel for 72 hours and generator vs power station.

You also need a clear sky: a tree or a badly oriented roof ruins the connection like a blackout. If the antenna cannot see the sky, Starlink is not your solution: first look at the best 4G emergency router.

5. The five most common mistakes when signing up

  • Signing up for Max out of habit. Priority is only noticeable at peak times; for a normal home, Residential 200 performs just as well for 30 €/mes less.
  • Reading only the rate. The real price is rate + rental: 45, 55 or 75 € a month. Always look at the total on your first bill.
  • Installing it without checking the sky. The app has an obstruction map: use it BEFORE paying and drilling into the roof.
  • Leaving the trial for the blackout. You have 30 days for a refund: test it on a normal day, in rain and with the TV on.
  • Forgetting where the power will come from. Without a plug, Starlink is a metal dish: first the power source and the battery that keeps it running.
