Who it's for. For households without air conditioning or with power cuts during a heatwave, and for anyone who has older people, babies or people with chronic illnesses at home. Spain has weeks above 40 degrees, and heatwaves are the natural hazard that causes the most deaths, almost all preventable.

When it isn't enough. It is not a beach or hiking kit: in those settings, what matters is shade, a hat and water. And it does not replace real prevention: if your home reaches 35 degrees at night, the kit helps, but the plan is to go somewhere cool.

The 5 typical mistakes (and how to avoid them)

  1. Drinking a lot of water on its own on days with heavy sweating, without salts. Hyponatremia (low salt) causes dizziness and confusion and is mistaken for heatstroke itself.
  2. Pulling down the blinds late. When the house is already at 33 degrees, opening south- and west-facing windows lets the heat in.
  3. Leaving anyone in a parked car: in 15 minutes the interior rises by more than 20 degrees. Pets too.
  4. Relying on a fan when it's over 35 degrees: it moves hot air and dehydrates you. From that point on you need to cool the body, not the air.
  5. Ignoring the older person who lives alone and says they're fine. Heatstroke in older people causes confusion, not thirst: they don't ask for help.

Quick guide

→ Before

  • Close blinds and sun-facing windows during the day
  • Drink water even if you are not thirsty; avoid alcohol and caffeine
  • Check on older neighbours and vulnerable people
  • Follow AEMET warnings and ES-Alert alerts on your mobile phone: the Basic Directive on adverse weather phenomena (BOE, 27/04/2026) establishes them as the main warning channel. The National Action Plan for Excess Temperatures is active from 1 June to 15 September

→ During

  • Stay in the coolest room in the house
  • Lukewarm showers, not cold ones
  • If you feel dizzy, have a headache or have dry, hot skin: it could be heatstroke, call 112

→ After

  • Check whether anyone's medication needs refrigeration and have a plan B
