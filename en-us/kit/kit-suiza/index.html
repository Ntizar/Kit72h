Quick guide

→ Before

  • Count the water using the Swiss rule: 9 liters per person, and add your pet and the heat in your area
  • Rotate the food: the reserve is normal food you eat and replace, not a separate cupboard that expires
  • Check the date on each water container and write it on the cap; bottled water expires too
  • Store chronic medication with the prescription in a cool place: it is the point no generic kit covers
  • Keep the cash in small notes and separate from your everyday wallet
  • Test the portable stove and radio once a year, with new batteries

→ During

  • Drink before you are thirsty: water is the first supply to fail and the hardest to improvise
  • Listen to the official radio twice a day; it is the channel that stays alive when the mobile network goes down
  • Cook during the day and with ventilation: a gas stove indoors kills with carbon monoxide if there is no air
  • Ration masks and disinfectant if you have to go out or clean contaminated areas
  • Do not open the fridge unless necessary: every opening loses hours of cold

→ After

  • Replace what you use within the same week, not 'someday': a half-done reserve is not a reserve
  • Check dates and note what expired unused (usually it is what you don't like eating)
  • Wash the stove, dry the batteries and put everything back where you first found it
