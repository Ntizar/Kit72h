The 5 typical mistakes (and how to avoid them)

  1. Starting with 20 varieties and a large garden. It gets abandoned in six weeks. Start with three things you eat: tomatoes, lettuce, herbs.
  2. Sowing by the calendar instead of by your climate: sow when your valley's temperature allows it, not when the table says.
  3. Using cheap discount-store potting mix or garden soil in pots: it compacts, doesn't drain and you kill the roots.
  4. Not having a guaranteed water supply: in a balcony vegetable garden, a weekend away without watering is a dead garden.
  5. Storing seeds without drying them and without a date: after two years nothing will germinate. Airtight jar, label, cool dark place.
  6. Throwing kitchen scraps in the bin: a 20 L compost bin costs less than 35 € and turns that waste into free fertilizer. The right ratio is two parts browns (dry leaves, cardboard) to one part greens (food scraps).
  7. Relying on insecticides without netting: whitefly reproduces in two weeks and insecticides kill bees. Cover first, treat afterwards.

Quick guide

→ Before

  • Look at your space: how many hours of direct sun it gets per day (at least 4 h for tomatoes; 2-3 h is enough for lettuce and herbs)
  • Start with what is easy and productive: lettuce, arugula, chard, herbs, radishes
  • Prepare the calendar: in Spain, spring sowing runs from February to April; autumn for spinach, garlic and onions
  • Set up a home composter: kitchen scraps feed the garden. A 20 L bucket with a tap costs 25-35 € and prevents smells
  • Cover windows with aphid-proof mesh (2x8 m, 10-20 €): without protection, whitefly and thrips can destroy a balcony garden in two weeks
  • Check your residents' association rules or housing portal: in some buildings (especially rentals), pots are banned on balconies or shared terraces. If they are allowed, ask about maximum weight and watering.
  • Buy pots with saucers: the saucer catches irrigation water and prevents stains on the floor of the entrance or communal terrace. Without a saucer, no one will let you keep the garden.

→ During

  • Water first thing in the morning or at dusk, never in direct sun
  • Loose, well-draining potting mix: a pot without holes is certain death for any plant
  • Add fertilizer every 2-3 weeks with compost or liquid fertilizer
  • If you use drip irrigation, check the drippers every week: they clog easily, and a dry plant is not noticeable until it is too late
  • Check the aphid mesh after storms: if it is torn, replace it. A 2 cm hole is enough for whitefly to get in
  • If you grow in pots, renew the potting mix each season: after one season it has lost nutrients and become compacted. About 5-8 € per pot and two new harvests.

→ After

  • Harvest from the outside (outer leaves) so the plant keeps producing
  • Save seeds from your best plants for the next cycle
  • Note down what worked and what did not: your garden notebook is your best tool
