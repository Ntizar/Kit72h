FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

What is a 72-hour emergency kit?

It is the set of supplies (water, non-perishable food, light, radio, first-aid kit and documents) that allows a household to be self-sufficient for 72 hours during a power outage, DANA or disaster. The European Union Preparedness Strategy of March 2025 recommends that all households have supplies for 72 hours. At Kit72h you'll find the complete list based on official sources, ready to add to your cart →

Where do I start if I have nothing?

Start with the essentials: water, light, radio and a first-aid kit. The 72-hour basic kit brings together the verified essentials and puts everything in your Amazon cart in one click. If the scenario you are worried about is a long power cut, add the blackout kit.

How much water should I store for an emergency?

3 litres per person per day for drinking alone, plus 2-4 additional litres for basic hygiene. For 72 hours, a family of 4 needs about 36 litres of drinking water. Water should be replaced every 6 months and kept away from sunlight and cleaning products. Water containers, storage and filters: all in the basic kit →

What should you always carry in your car?

The basics are the same almost everywhere in Europe: a hi-vis vest for every occupant (stored inside the cabin, not in the boot), a warning triangle, a first-aid kit and a torch. In winter add warm clothes and a blanket; on long trips, water and a charged power bank. Check your own country's highway code before you rely on it: Spain, for example, has required a connected V-16 beacon instead of triangles since 1 January 2026.

What should you do in the first 30 minutes of a blackout?

Do not call 911 unless it is a real emergency (use messages), turn on a battery-powered or hand-crank radio to get information from official sources, unplug sensitive appliances before the power comes back on, use a flashlight instead of candles and conserve your phone battery by switching to airplane mode. If the outage is going to last: blackout kit and power kit.

What food is suitable for 72 hours without a fridge?

Non-perishable food: cans, jars, energy bars, nuts and freeze-dried foods, with menus designed for 3 days without cooking or with alternative cooking. The 30-day kit expands your pantry if you want extra buffer →
