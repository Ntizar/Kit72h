The 7 official resources to save on your phone and on paper (2026)

📅 2026-08-31 · ⏱ 2 min min read

In a crisis, the difference between acting well and acting late is having the right source at hand. And distrusting everything else: during the 2025 blackout, dozens of hoaxes circulated. Save these seven channels now — in your phone's favourites and, even better, written down on paper in your emergency folder.

The 7 essentials

  • 112: emergencies across the whole EU, free, no credit needed, and it works even if your operator has no coverage. Save it, but also know the number by heart.
  • AlertCops (official app of the Ministry of the Interior): public safety alerts and geolocated emergencies in your area. Download it now; in the middle of a crisis, app stores fail too.
  • AEMET (aemet.es and its app): weather warnings by province. In a DANA, the AEMET red warning arrives hours before the water does.
  • Protección Civil (proteccioncivil.es): self-preparation guides by risk and, during a crisis, guidance on what to do. It is the source from which the lists on this website are drawn.
  • Tráfico DGT (app and official Twitter/X): road conditions, closures and diversions in real time — essential if you have to move or evacuate.
  • Bulletin and FM radio: a battery-powered radio tuned to national and regional stations is the only medium that survives a total network collapse.
  • Town hall and regional government: every municipality has its emergency plan and its channels (regional 112, municipal alerts). Find them now, not when the siren goes off.

On paper, because phones die

Write this down in your folder: 112, the Protección Civil phone number for your municipality, your health centre, two contacts outside your area, and your home insurance policy number. Half a page in handwriting that works with your phone at 0%.
