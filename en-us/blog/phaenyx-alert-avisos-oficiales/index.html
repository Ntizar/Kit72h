Phaenyx Alert: your 72h kit now warns you before the storm

📅 2026-11-25 · ⏱ 4 min min read

An emergency kit is a logistics promise: water, light, radio and paperwork ready on the day you need them. But there is one piece no kit can pack: knowing what is happening and whether it affects you. That is where Phaenyx Alert comes in, the alert app Kit72h has just partnered with as one of only three associate shops in its region.

The idea is simple: you keep buying your gear where you always have, and every order that comes from Kit72h gifts a full year of the Family plan (worth US$19) with code KT72H. No card, no small print, no change to your habits.

What Phaenyx Alert is, in two sentences

It is an app that matches official alerts against the places you care about: your home, the house in the village, your children's school or a relative's care home. When an official alert touches one of those points, your phone rings. It is not a chat group or yet another weather map: it filters the noise and leaves only the warning that actually concerns you.

The Family plan adds three people and the “I'm safe” button, which tells the whole family with a single tap. For anyone with kids at school, elderly parents two streets away or a holiday house 300 kilometres out, that button is worth more than any group chat.

The kit solves logistics; the app solves information

The EU Preparedness Strategy recommends that households keep provisions for at least 72 hours, and civil protection authorities insist on the same: basic self-sufficiency, documents to hand and a family plan talked through before the emergency arrives. The kit covers the “meanwhile”. The app covers the “what on earth is happening?”, the question everyone asks at once and where the first minutes of any emergency get lost.

“I'm safe”: the call you no longer have to make

During a blackout or an evacuation the mobile network saturates just when the most people are trying to call. One tap on “I'm safe” reaches the whole family without tying up a line, and each person can confirm their own status separately. That is the difference between two hours of calling a number that never answers and knowing, in ten seconds, that your people are fine.
