Kit 72h or 30-day kit: which one is yours (spoiler: the first one) (2026)

📅 2026-08-31 · ⏱ 2 min min read

The European Union asks households to have 72 hours of self-sufficiency. Why 72? Because that is the typical time it takes emergency services to restore the basics in a serious crisis: drinking water, cleared roads, communications. But some crises are not resolved in three days. Let's compare the two levels.

The 72-hour kit: for 95% of scenarios

DANA, grid blackout, water outage, storm, snowfall: in almost all cases that have occurred in Spain in recent decades, three days of water, food, light and information get you through. It costs 100-150 €, fits in a backpack and is maintained with a check every 6 months. If you don't have one yet, this is your kit. It is broken down in the basic kit and in its variants by scenario (DANA, blackout, car…).

The 30-day kit: for the extreme case

A DANA that isolates your area for a week, a multi-day European blackout, a lockdown like 2020. Here we are no longer talking about a backpack but a pantry: 180 liters of water for two people, non-perishable food for weeks, a way to cook without electricity and a proper first-aid kit. It is more expensive (300-500 €) and needs active rotation. The complete, calculated version is in the 30-day kit.

Why the order matters

  • The 72-hour kit covers the first hit of any crisis, including long ones: the first three days are always critical.
  • The 30-day kit without the 72-hour kit is poorly designed: whoever has a pantry but no radio, flashlight or information radio fails at the first step.
  • Buying in reverse (lots of food, zero preparation) is the most common mistake: rice pantries with the phone battery at 5%.

The recommended progression

  1. Basic 72-hour kit + battery-powered radio (this weekend).
  2. Document folder + cash (one afternoon).
  3. Build the pantry up to 2 weeks, then 30 days.
  4. Vegetable garden and sprouts as long-term reinforcement.

Both kits are calculated for 2 people and with real, verified products: 72 hours · 30 days.
